01

What is Phishing?

Understanding the threat is the first step toward preventing it.

🎣

Phishing

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers impersonate trusted individuals or organizations to trick victims into revealing sensitive information or performing unsafe actions.

📧

Email Phishing

Attackers send deceptive emails containing suspicious links, attachments or requests for sensitive information.

🌐

Fake Websites

Fraudulent websites are designed to look like legitimate services and may attempt to collect usernames, passwords or payment information.

02

Recognize the Warning Signs

Look carefully before clicking a link or responding to a request.

01

Urgent Language

Messages may pressure you to act immediately or threaten account suspension.

02

Suspicious Sender

Check the complete sender address instead of trusting only the displayed name.

03

Unexpected Links

Hover over links and verify the destination before opening them.

04

Requests for Secrets

Be suspicious of unexpected requests for passwords, OTPs or financial information.

05

Unexpected Attachments

Do not open unexpected attachments without verifying the sender and context.

06

Spelling & Grammar

Unusual wording, spelling mistakes or inconsistent branding can be warning signs.

03

Phishing Email Example

Use multiple clues together when evaluating a suspicious message.

⚠️ Suspicious Email Security Alert

From: account-security@secure-example.com

Subject: URGENT: Your account will be suspended!

Hello User,

We detected unusual activity on your account. You must verify your account immediately to prevent suspension.

Please complete verification immediately.

Security Team

⚠️ Training example: Never click suspicious links just because a message creates urgency. Verify the sender and destination independently.
04

Social Engineering

Attackers often manipulate human behavior rather than technical systems.

⏰

Urgency

Creating pressure so the target makes a quick decision without verification.

👤

Impersonation

Pretending to be a manager, bank, service provider or another trusted person.

🎁

Rewards

Offering fake prizes, benefits or opportunities to encourage unsafe actions.

🔐

Fear

Using threats such as account closure or security incidents to influence behavior.

05

How to Stay Safe

Follow these practical security habits.

1

Pause

Do not react immediately to unexpected urgent messages.

2

Verify

Confirm the sender and request through a trusted communication channel.

3

Inspect

Check links, domains, attachments and message details carefully.

4

Protect

Use strong unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication where available.

5

Report

Report suspicious messages to the appropriate security or IT team.

07

Quick Security Checklist

Before trusting a message, ask yourself:

✓ Do I recognize the sender?
✓ Is the request expected?
✓ Does the link lead to the correct domain?
✓ Is the message creating unnecessary urgency?
✓ Is it asking for sensitive information?
✓ Can I verify the request independently?
